Wearables & Fitness Tracking
Worth Understanding First

Tracking is not the goal. It is the tool.

The best fitness tracker is the one that gives you information you actually act on. If seeing your sleep score makes you go to bed earlier, it is working. If it just makes you anxious about numbers, it is not. Choose the tracker that fits the behavior change you are trying to make, not the one with the most features.

Honest Answers

Questions we actually get

Q. What is the best weight loss program for someone who has tried everything?
If you have genuinely tried multiple programs and regained the weight, the answer is probably not a better diet. It is likely a behavioral approach (Noom), a medical conversation (Calibrate), or a more honest look at sleep and stress, which are the factors most programs never address. Start with whichever of those three feels most relevant to your actual situation, not your ideal situation.
Q. WHOOP or Oura Ring? Which should I buy?
It depends what you want the data for. WHOOP is better for active people who want training and recovery guidance, it tells you how hard to push today. Oura is better for people prioritizing sleep quality and overall health monitoring, especially if a discrete form factor matters. If you train hard and want recovery data, WHOOP. If you want the best sleep tracker available, Oura.
Q. Is AG1 actually worth the price?
For most people, yes, with a caveat. If you eat an extremely varied, whole-food-heavy diet consistently, you may not need it. If your diet is imperfect on a regular basis (most people), a daily comprehensive supplement that covers micronutrient gaps is genuinely worth $79 per month. The question to ask is: what is the cost of the health gaps you are currently carrying without knowing it?
Q. What is the Popcycle Score based on?
We score on four factors: effectiveness (does it work, based on research and verified reviews), value (fair price for what you get), quality (ingredients, build, credentials), and how confidently we would recommend it to someone we care about. Scores above 90 are reserved for products we consider genuinely excellent with minimal reservations.
