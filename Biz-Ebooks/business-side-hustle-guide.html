Honest Answers

Questions we actually get

Q. Which tool should I buy first?
It depends entirely on what stage you are at. If you have something to sell and no way to sell it online, Shopify first. If you have an audience but no way to communicate with them directly, Kit first. If you have expertise and no platform to deliver it, Kajabi or Teachable first. If you have none of the above and you are still in the "idea" phase, start with HubSpot free (zero cost, zero risk) and a Canva Pro account to start producing content. The mistake is buying tools before you know what problem they are solving.
Q. Do I need a CRM?
If you have more than 20 active prospects or clients at any given time, yes. If your sales cycle is longer than a single conversation, yes. If you have ever lost a deal because you forgot to follow up, definitively yes. HubSpot's free tier handles almost every small business CRM need with no expiration date, so the barrier to starting is essentially zero.
Q. Are sales funnels still relevant in 2026?
Yes, though the terminology has evolved. The underlying concept — a deliberate, structured path from first awareness to purchase — is more relevant than ever because the attention environment is more competitive than ever. ClickFunnels and Leadpages both represent this category well. The question is not whether you need a funnel, but whether you need dedicated funnel software or whether your existing tools (email platform, website builder) are sufficient for your current volume.
Q. Kajabi or Teachable — which should I choose?
The decision comes down to where you are in the business. Teachable is the right choice if you are launching your first course and want the simplest possible path from "course content" to "paying students." Kajabi is the right choice if you are building a knowledge business as a primary income source and want everything — courses, community, email, coaching, podcasts, website — in one system. The price difference ($39 vs $149/month) reflects that difference in scope.
Q. Do you earn commissions from these recommendations?
Yes, and we want to be transparent about it: this category has some of the highest affiliate commissions available in any market, which creates an obvious conflict of interest for most review sites. Our Popcycle Scores are set independently of commission rates — Shopify's $150+ referral fee did not make it score higher, and a product that pays lower commissions can score higher if it is genuinely the better tool. We turned down several products in this category with strong affiliate programs because we would not actually recommend them. They are not on this page.
