How to Read This

Not all online GLP-1
providers are equal

Getting a GLP-1 prescribed online can be genuinely convenient and more affordable. But the market has exploded, and it now spans everything from physician-led obesity clinics to no-frills operations that hand you a prescription and disappear. The gap in quality is enormous — so we vetted them.

Two things shape our rankings. First, we favor providers offering brand-name, FDA-approved medications (Wegovy, Zepbound, and the newer oral options), increasingly available directly through partnerships with the drugmakers. Second, we weigh the quality of care — whether there's a real medical evaluation, lab work when appropriate, ongoing support, and honest pricing — because a GLP-1 journey is a year-plus commitment, not a one-time transaction.

We're transparent about one more thing: Popcycle may earn a commission if you choose a provider through our links. That's how we keep the lights on. But it never changes the rankings — the scores below reflect our honest assessment of care quality and value, and we flag every caveat, including for providers whose programs pay us well.

Read This Before You Choose

The compounded-vs-branded question matters more than the price.

Many online providers push lower-cost "compounded" semaglutide or tirzepatide. These aren't FDA-approved and haven't been tested for safety, effectiveness, or quality the way the branded drugs have — and regulators have flagged concerns and issued warnings across this space. Compounded medication has a legitimate role in specific situations, but it isn't a generic equivalent. We favor branded, approved options and full transparency, and we flag providers that lean heavily on compounded products or cut clinical corners.

  • Compounded GLP-1s are not FDA-approved or quality-tested like branded drugs
  • A real provider should require a proper evaluation — and often lab work
  • Watch for red flags: no video visit, no labs, undisclosed compounding pharmacy
  • Beware "first-month" bait pricing that jumps sharply after you enroll
  • Whatever you choose, the prescribing decision belongs with a qualified clinician

The Trust Anchors

Straight from the source: NovoCare & LillyDirect

Worth knowing even though they aren't affiliate plays: the drugmakers now sell directly. Novo Nordisk's NovoCare Pharmacy offers self-pay Wegovy, and Eli Lilly's LillyDirect offers Zepbound — often at the lowest legitimate self-pay prices, and the definition of FDA-approved and authentic. Several featured providers above (Ro, LifeMD, Hims, WeightWatchers, Noom) plug into these same drugmaker programs. They're the reference point every other price and product should be measured against.
